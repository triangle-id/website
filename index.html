How it works

Turn your CV into a VC.

A verifiable credential (VC), signed by employers and held by you. Provable to anyone in seconds.

01

Employer issues

Your former employer signs a credential confirming your role, dates, and title, all from a simple dashboard.

02

You hold it

The credential is yours, locked to your device with a passkey. No central wallet, no third party in the way.

03

Anyone verifies

Share your credential anywhere. Platforms verify it in seconds, with no contact back to the employer.
